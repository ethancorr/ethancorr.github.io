Speaking & Outreach

Track record

Community involvement

I'm involved in Milwaukee's data science and STEM community, and hope to get even more involved going forward. Recent talks include "Masters of the Mundane: Unveiling STEM Potential through Everyday Expertise" at the 2025 SySTEMnow Conference, "Battery Bot: Engineering Powered by AI" at a Clarios Americas regional tech talk, and "Equivalent Circuit Models & Mathematics in Industry" for a Marquette student success seminar. For several years I led "Engineering Challenge Day," an annual STEM event for middle schoolers at the MSOE STEM Center, and I attended Data-Driven Wisconsin in 2026. I was also part of the winning team at the 2026 Discovery World Hackathon, hosted by the Northwestern Mutual Data Science Institute (NMDSI). I enjoy engaging with audiences that range from students and career-changers to industry professionals and civic groups.
